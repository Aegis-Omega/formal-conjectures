Reading:

- `NP = 900`, `ε = 1`, `κ = 60` with boxes `5e-11` (`τ_k`, `k ≤ 150`), `5e-10` (rows above) and
  `4e-11` (diagonal) keeps a margin of `8.2e-9`, against `1.02e-8` with boxes of `5e-13`.
- Boxes four times wider (`2e-10`) still pass, with `1.4e-9`.
- `NP = 700`, `κ = 66` passes with `2.4e-9` at boxes of `5e-11`; it is the thinner choice.

So the `τ` and diagonal boxes of the port need to be about 200 times tighter than at 11/8
(`1e-8`, `1e-7`, `8.5e-9`), not 20,000 times. The LDL certificate was not rerun on these matrices.
